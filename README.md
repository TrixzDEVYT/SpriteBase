🌐 **SpriteBase** is a community-driven, multilingual platform built for Fortnite Sprite collectors and traders around the world. Whether you're just starting your collection or you're hunting down those last few missing Sprites, SpriteBase gives you a dedicated place to organize, discover, and trade.

📦 **Manage your collection**
Keep track of the Sprites you own, organize your collection, and easily see which variants are already part of your collection and which ones you're still missing.

🔎 **Discover Sprites**
Browse through the Sprite catalog and explore different variants, rarities, and collectibles. Find new Sprites to add to your wishlist and discover ones you may not have encountered before.

🔄 **Find trades**
Looking for a specific Sprite? Find other collectors who have what you need and discover potential trades based on the Sprites you're looking for and the ones you're willing to exchange.

🌎 **Trade with the community**
Connect with collectors from around the world and make it easier to find the right people for your trades. SpriteBase is designed to bring the Fortnite Sprite collecting community together in one place.

🌐 **Built for everyone**
With multilingual support, SpriteBase aims to make collecting and trading accessible to a global community, regardless of where you're from.

✨ **Collect what you love. Discover what's missing. Find your next trade.**

SpriteBase is more than a collection tracker. It's a growing hub for Fortnite Sprite collectors, built around discovering, collecting, and trading with the community.
